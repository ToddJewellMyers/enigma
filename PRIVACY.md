# Enigma privacy notice

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Enigma provides account-based Kanban project management. This notice describes the information handled by the hosted Enigma service and its desktop applications.

## Information Enigma handles

- **Account information:** the email address supplied during registration and a one-way password hash. Enigma does not store the original password.
- **User content:** workspace, board, column, and card information entered into the service, including titles, descriptions, priorities, and due dates.
- **Service information:** standard operational logs such as request paths, timestamps, status information, client IP information available to the hosting platform, and diagnostic trace identifiers.

## Why the information is used

Enigma uses this information to authenticate accounts, provide Kanban functionality, isolate each account's data, maintain service security, diagnose failures, and monitor availability.

## Sharing and selling

Enigma does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to operate the service, including the hosting and database provider, only as necessary to provide and secure Enigma. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

## Storage and security

Hosted Enigma data is stored in PostgreSQL. Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS in production, and application data is restricted by authenticated account ownership. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Desktop terminal

The Enigma Community desktop edition includes an optional local terminal. Terminal processes and commands run on the user's Mac and are not transmitted to the Enigma service by the terminal feature. The Mac App Store edition does not include this terminal.

## Retention and user choices

Workspace, board, and card data remains until it is deleted through Enigma or the associated account is removed by the service operator. Account deletion and automated data export are not yet self-service features. Users may request assistance from the repository owner while dedicated support channels are being established.

## Children

Enigma is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used to submit children's personal information.

## Changes

This notice may be updated as Enigma changes. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

## Contact

Until a dedicated support address is published, privacy questions can be opened through the Enigma GitHub repository at `https://github.com/ToddJewellMyers/enigma/issues`.
