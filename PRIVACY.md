# Sweet Mahogany Boards privacy notice

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Sweet Mahogany Boards provides account-based Kanban project management. This notice describes the information handled by the hosted Sweet Mahogany Boards service and its desktop applications.

## Information Sweet Mahogany Boards handles

- **Account information:** the email address supplied during registration and a one-way password hash. Sweet Mahogany Boards does not store the original password.
- **User content:** workspace, board, column, and card information entered into the service, including titles, descriptions, priorities, and due dates.
- **Service information:** standard operational logs such as request paths, timestamps, status information, client IP information available to the hosting platform, and diagnostic trace identifiers.

## Why the information is used

Sweet Mahogany Boards uses this information to authenticate accounts, provide Kanban functionality, isolate each account's data, maintain service security, diagnose failures, and monitor availability.

## Sharing and selling

Sweet Mahogany Boards does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to operate the service, including the hosting and database provider, only as necessary to provide and secure Sweet Mahogany Boards. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

## Storage and security

Hosted Sweet Mahogany Boards data is stored in PostgreSQL. Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS in production, and application data is restricted by authenticated account ownership. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Desktop terminal

The Sweet Mahogany Boards Community desktop edition includes an optional local terminal. Terminal processes and commands run on the user's Mac and are not transmitted to the Sweet Mahogany Boards service by the terminal feature. The Mac App Store edition does not include this terminal.

## Retention and user choices

Workspace, board, and card data remains until it is deleted through Sweet Mahogany Boards or the associated account is removed. Signed-in users can download a JSON copy of their account data and permanently delete their account from **Account settings**. Account deletion removes the account and its workspaces, boards, columns, and cards.

## Children

Sweet Mahogany Boards is not directed to children under 13 and should not be used to submit children's personal information.

## Changes

This notice may be updated as Sweet Mahogany Boards changes. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

## Contact

Until a dedicated support address is published, privacy questions can be opened through the Sweet Mahogany Boards GitHub repository at `https://github.com/ToddJewellMyers/enigma/issues`.
